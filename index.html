About Our Solution

The problem

Costly or uncertain visits

A worrying symptom usually means an expensive clinic trip or a confusing search, with no way to tell which one the moment actually calls for.

Contradictory advice

Forums and generic chatbots give confident answers with no way for an owner to check where they came from.

Unverified professionals

Anyone can claim to be a vet online, and owners have no reliable way to tell who actually is one.

How VETeran works

Two retrieval pipelines feed a single safety gate: cited rules set the minimum urgency, and retrieved evidence explains it.

Hybrid RAG · Clinical triage

Strict rules, searchable evidence

Species-aware symptom answers go through a strict rule lane that catches red flags and a search lane that pulls and reranks matching literature passages. Every verdict links the exact passages behind it, and no red flag is ever downgraded.

Multimodal RAG · Visual triage

Photos checked against real cases

A vision model embeds the owner's photo and finds the closest cases in trusted veterinary literature. Low-similarity matches are flagged, never guessed, and a photo can only raise concern, never lower an urgency level.

Roadmap

Visual symptom triage

Image embeddings, similar-case search, confidence thresholds and fallback.

Milestone: photo signal live in verdicts

Clinical validation

Veterinarian review of verdicts and a pilot with partner clinics.

Milestone: clinical-grade release
